Wedding Package Information


The Kavya Sharma Photography team offers three wedding packages starting from $2900.00.  We offer a five, eight and ten and twelve hour package, with the option of adding additional hours to customise the coverage of your wedding day. You have the option to further customise the coverage of your package by adding a second photographer for a minimum of five hours.

With every enquiry you receive a no obligation free initial consultation over phone or zoom discussing your needs and options for your special day. We will have an in depth discussion on the timing and construction of your wedding day, we make sure you are utilising the best light of the day and we give you tips to ensure your special day runs smoothly.

After your images are carefully edited, you’ll receive a personalized online gallery to easily download and share with friends and family. While digital files are essential, I believe there’s something truly special about holding your favorite images in print. That’s why every wedding package includes a selection of professionally printed photos, ready to be displayed and cherished in your home.

If you're looking for a beautiful way to showcase your wedding photos, explore our Boutique Albums here. These albums make timeless keepsakes, perfect for displaying in your home or preserving for special moments with family and friends in the years ahead.

If you're planning a small, intimate wedding or an elopement and would like to know more about how we can make your day unforgettable, please find additional details here. 

Our first meeting


Choosing a wedding photographer is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your big day. That's why I offer a free, no-obligation consultation, available by phone or Zoom. We’ll chat about your expectations, style, timing, lighting, and any other questions you have to make sure everything aligns perfectly with your vision.

What to expect


Once you decide to book your wedding day with the Kavya Sharma Photography team, you’ll receive a welcome package by mail, filled with my most-trusted supplier recommendations and top ten bridal tips. My goal is to offer you all the guidance you need to make wedding planning as smooth as possible, so you feel supported every step of the way. I’ll share helpful emails with stories and insights gathered from my brides’ experiences, giving you inspiration and useful advice. When your special day arrives, I’ll be there right beside you, sharing in your joy with a calm, approachable presence, creating a comfortable, safe space for you to be your very best self.
