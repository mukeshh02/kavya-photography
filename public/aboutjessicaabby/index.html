Kavya Sharma

Destination Wedding & Intimate Portrait Photographer


Having had the incredible opportunity to photograph over 350 weddings across India and worldwide, I have developed a deep appreciation for showcasing the intimate moments and emotions that make your special day truly unforgettable. It is an absolute gift to be able to do what I love and be a part of such joyful celebrations. 

 One of my greatest strengths as a photographer is my ability to remain calm and stress-free, even in the midst of the wedding day chaos. With many years of experience under my belt, I am well-equipped to assist you in  many ways throughout your wedding planning journey. Whether you have questions or concerns leading up to the big day, or need someone reliable and experienced to rely on during the event itself, I am here to support you every step of the way.

Thinking of inspiration, traveling is one of my main passions. I find that immersing myself in different cultures and landscapes truly feeds my soul. Whether it's capturing the breathtaking beauty of a destination wedding or discovering hidden gems in my own country, I'm always up for an adventure.

Performance art holds a special place in my heart as it provides me with endless inspiration. The creativity and emotion expressed through dance, theatre, and music never fails to leave me in awe. 

My last obsession is gardening. There's something incredibly fulfilling about nurturing plants and watching them flourish. It's a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

If you are interested in learning more about my work or would like to schedule a consultation to discuss your wedding photography needs, please do not hesitate to reach out to me. I would be honoured to become a part of your special day and capture the beautiful moments that will forever be cherished.
