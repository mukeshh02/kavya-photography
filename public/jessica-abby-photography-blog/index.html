FOREVER YOURS


Hi Lovers!


I’m Kavya Sharma, and I specialise in photographing people in love.

With a heart for storytelling, I’m passionate about capturing your love with joy, energy, and those genuine, laughter-filled moments.

I’m endlessly inspired by real connection and those sweet moments where your love truly shines. Whether it’s a quiet glance, a burst of laughter, or that just-married glow, I’m here to document the magic honestly and beautifully.

Based in Melbourne, I regularly travel across Victoria and South Australia, with camera in hand and heart wide open. I photograph weddings, engagements, proposals & registry weddings.

I know being in front of the camera can feel a little intimidating and that’s okay. My approach is warm, relaxed, and encouraging, so you can feel completely at ease.

Kavya Sharma x
