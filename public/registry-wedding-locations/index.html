Your intimate wedding day is a truly special and unique occasion, and choosing the perfect photo locations nearby can help make everything flow effortlessly. 

Parliament Building

Just a short walk away, these grand steps are iconic and perfect for creating timeless, elegant shots. The historic architecture adds sophistication and grandeur. This is the Number one location for incredible photos! 

Treasury Gardens

Right next to the Melbourne Registry Office, Treasury Gardens offers lush greenery, winding paths, and cityscape views in the background. It's a classic spot with a serene ambiance

Chinatown

Melbourne's Chinatown offers vibrant lanterns, narrow alleys, and a unique cultural atmosphere. The color and energy here make for interesting, bold photos.

The Old Treasury Building

The registry office is in this historic building it boasts intricate architecture and columns, adding a vintage feel. The facade, steps, and arches are ideal for romantic portraits. Before or after your ceremony.

The Hotel Windsor

The Hotel Windsor is a landmark of classic elegance with its Victorian architecture, grand staircases, and luxurious interiors. Its ornate details and old-world charm make it ideal for capturing timeless, sophisticated photos.

Fitzroy Gardens

This nearby garden is filled with scenic spots, including the Conservatory, vibrant flower beds, and lovely pathways. It’s an ideal choice for couples who want a mix of natural and structured elements.

 Pellegrini’s Espresso Bar

This iconic Melbourne cafe brings vintage Italian vibes, perfect for adding a touch of authenticity and character to your photos. The neon sign, cozy interior, and retro counter create an intimate, nostalgic backdrop, capturing Melbourne’s cafe culture.
