The Beauty of the

Old Treasury Building

Constructed in the 1850s, the Old Treasury Building is a stunning example of 19th-century Renaissance Revival architecture. Its grand façade and historic rooms provide a classic yet contemporary backdrop for your wedding photography. The soft natural light that floods the space and the ornate details of the interior ensure that your photos will have a warm, rich, and timeless feel.

Not only does the building itself offer a variety of beautiful settings for your ceremony and portraits, but its proximity to nearby Melbourne landmarks, like Fitzroy Gardens, Treasury Gardens and my favourite the Parliament Building. This allows for a variety of backdrops that add depth and beauty to your wedding gallery.

Registry Wedding Photography

Old Treasury Building, Melbourne

The Old Treasury Building in Melbourne is an iconic venue for couples who want an intimate yet stunning backdrop for their registry wedding. With its rich history, grand architecture, and central location, it has become one of Melbourne’s most beloved spots for couples seeking a beautiful, meaningful place to say “I do.”

As a wedding photographer, I understand the importance of capturing the subtle emotions and unique details that make your day truly special. The Old Treasury Building offers a perfect blend of elegance and charm, creating an ideal setting for timeless, romantic wedding photography. Whether you're planning a small ceremony with close family and friends or a ceremony with witnesses only, this venue has everything you need to make your registry wedding unforgettable.

You can find more information on what ceremonies the Victorian Registry Office offers - https://www.bdm.vic.gov.au/our-wedding-ceremonies

Capturing the Intimacy of Your Registry Wedding

At the heart of registry weddings is the idea of simplicity and intimacy. My goal is to reflect that in your photography by focusing on the authentic connection between you and your partner. With a smaller guest list and fewer distractions, I can capture those candid, heartfelt moments that might otherwise be missed in a larger wedding setting. From the nervous excitement before the ceremony to the joy and laughter as you walk down the aisle as a newly married couple, my approach is to be both creative and unobtrusive, ensuring that you feel comfortable and relaxed.

Your registry wedding at the Old Treasury Building will be a day to remember, and I’m here to make sure those memories are preserved beautifully through thoughtful, elegant photography. If you're looking for a photographer who understands the unique charm of this historic Melbourne venue and can capture the essence of your special day, I would love to be part of your journey.
