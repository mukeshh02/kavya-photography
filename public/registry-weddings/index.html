FAQ for Micro Weddings, Registry Weddings & Elopements


What exactly is a Micro Wedding? 

This is a far more intimate and relaxed affair with typically 5-30 guests. It still features all the time-honored traditions that make a wedding so beautiful but on a much smaller scale. Most micro weddings are held in smaller venues and most popular the Melbourne registry office.

What are the minimum hours of coverage for a registry, elopement, or micro wedding? 

Your wedding day is special and unique. That is why my micro wedding package is tailor-made for your specific event. I start my micro wedding packages with a base of 1 hour coverage of photography, you have the ability to add hours if you need. 

Have you photographed at the Registry Office in Melbourne before? 

Yes! I have successfully photographed many weddings at the Melbourne Registry Office.

How do I book your services? 

Everything starts when you send a simple email with the date and time of your upcoming ceremony. I will be happy to help answer more questions and iron out any details for your celebration.

Are registry weddings common?

Absolutely! Intimate weddings have become increasingly popular, with many couples choosing a smaller celebration that's focused on what matters most, their love and commitment to one another.

A smaller guest list often means less stress, more meaningful time with your loved ones, and the opportunity to enjoy the day at a relaxed pace. Whether you're sharing a beautiful meal together, exploring Melbourne for portraits, or simply soaking in the moment, intimate weddings create space for genuine connection.

Many couples also choose a registry wedding before a larger overseas celebration, or as a way to celebrate with family and friends who aren't able to travel internationally. Whatever your reason, your wedding day is just as special and deserves to be beautifully documented.
