Should we have a meeting? I always suggest we meet via a phone conversation or in the comfort of your own home over zoom, this is an opportunity to get to know each other, and we can discuss the plan for your big day. Every wedding is different, so we talk through a unique plan tailored specifically for your wedding day


Tips for the camera shy? That’s ok, 80% of my clients all feel the same. After 15 years of photographing people, I understand that is all about both of you feeling safe enough to be yourself and have a laugh. I provide you as much or little direction as you need for your to feel comfortable.

Will you customise a package? Yes, I am more than happy to customise the perfect package specifically for your wedding day.

Do you do destination weddings? My bags are already packed ready to photograph you in front of a new horizon. 

What do your prices start at? Wedding packages start at $3800.00

Do you have a weekday discount? Yes, all wedding packages booked to take place on Monday - Thursday receive a 10% discount. (Please note this offer is not available for weddings taking place in December.)

How long does it take to edit the images? The turn around on your digital wedding files is 2-6 weeks. 

Do you offer Albums?  I sure do and you can find out more information here

Do you charge for travel? If you are outside of Metro Melbourne there will be an additional charge, these would be discussed in pre-wedding meetings and charged accordingly. 

How many images do I receive? You receive a curated collection of images. All the images are edited in high resolution with no watermarks. I average 80 edited images per hour to ensure that no moments are missed. You receive your images high resolution which are print ready for you to print off freely. You also receive all your images low resolution ready for social media and emailing. Ready to share straight away!

When is the final payment due? Your final payment is due 7 business days (1 week) before your wedding.

I want to book, what now? Just send me a confirmation email stating that you would like me to photograph your special day, we will book in a phone call or zoom. I then will then send my Terms and Conditions to you with all the deposit and payment details enclosed. I require a $500 deposit to confirm your wedding date.
