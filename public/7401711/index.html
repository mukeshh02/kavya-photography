The boutique wedding albums are a tangible keepsake with exceptional quality and design. They are all personally designed by Kavya Sharma making each wedding album unique to the married couple. The Boutique Album is a flush mount album printed on fine art, archival paper. The pages are creased down the centre of the page ensuring a lay flat design that provides an interrupted view of each album spread. The wedding albums are made in Brunswick, Victoria. Each album includes white cotton gloves, an album care guide and a certificate of authenticity. 

Why not show off the most beautiful day of your life? Albums make for great coffee table decor at your home! Guests can flip through the pages of your wedding album and relive one of the most glamorous events of your life. Or alternatively you can keep it safe for your children and grandchildren to cherish. Digital files are excellent to archive, but if you would like to evoke emotion from your wedding day there is nothing quite like physical photographs you can hold in your hand.

We have a range of material covers available for you to choose and we have the 10x10 inch available. There are 15 double page spreads equalling to 30 stunning pages printed on  Canson® Infinity Photo Lustre paper 310gsm exhibiting your special day to perfection.
