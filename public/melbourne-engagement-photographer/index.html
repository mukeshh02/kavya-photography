Engagement Photography

An engagement session is the perfect opportunity to celebrate this exciting chapter of your lives while becoming comfortable in front of the camera before your wedding day.

 

EFFORTLESS BEAUTY

Melina & Ben

Melina and Ben were an absolute delight to photograph. Every moment felt effortless as we walked the streets of Melbourne CBD.

GLAMOUR

Toni & Tom

Toni made contact and discussed her idea for their portrait session. With three dress changes and the most magical location at Mont Du Soleil we captured the the glamorous images she was after.

FUN FUN FUN

Jemma & Brendan

Jemma & Brendan's portrait session was filled to the brim with laughter.  A stroll around St Kilda Gardens with a dress change and we made many magical memories to cherish forever!

BIRTHDAY GIFT

Amy & Damien

Amy surprised Damien on his birthday, what a wonderful gift of stunning memories to keep forever! Black on black for these lovers, a cute red rose bouquet while sipping champagne.

LETS CELEBRATE

Tom & Georgia

Engagement shoot on Valentines Day, how romantic! We made the most gorgeous memories for you both on the coast of Ocean Grove. 

VOGUE

Gracie & Jordan

Gracie and Jordan began their engagement session in the beautifully lit Salon of the National Gallery of Victoria, where the timeless art and soft natural light set the stage for elegant portraits. From there, they made their way to the striking façade of the Parliament building.

ROCK N ROLL

Hattie & Wade

We started with the outfit, piece by piece. Sourced our location. Met at sunset - laughed, loved and captured a series of images that reflect their stylish flair. Fur, flowers and over flowing champagne. What more do you need?! 

FLINDERS

Hoa & Rob

I will never get sick of photographing at this spectacular location on the coast of Flinders, Mornington Peninsula. It's by far one of my favourite spots to photograph portrait sessions.

YOUNG LOVE

Des & Stm 

This cute AF series reminds me of young love over and over again! It was as simple as meeting at Port Melbourne beach and capturing the love unfold in front of my lens. 

SPONTANEOUS & LOVING

Helen & James

It may have been a freezing night on the Port Melbourne beach but hearts were warmed by all the love and laughter. Not to mention the sparks that were flying between lovebirds Helen and James. 

ADVENTUROUS SOULS

Henry & Shirley

Henry and Shirley chose the dreamy coastline of Port Melbourne for their beach session — a place that perfectly matched their vision. The evening unfolded beautifully, and we lingered well past sunset, capturing moody light as day turned to night.

EFFORTLESSLY COOL

Alex & Jake

I loved working with creative souls, Alex and Jake, who granted me complete photographic freedom for their session. I took inspiration from their artistic backgrounds and shot against a cityscape sunset for a dramatic, creative and romantic atmosphere.
