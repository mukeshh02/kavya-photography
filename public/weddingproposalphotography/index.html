A wedding proposal is one of the most romantic and unforgettable moments in a couple's journey towards marriage. The lead up to a wedding proposal is an exhilarating time for both the proposer and the proposee. It's a moment filled with anticipation, excitement, and nerves as everything comes together for the big moment. As a wedding photographer, I understand the importance of capturing this special moment and preserving it for a lifetime. From scouting the perfect location to coordinating with the proposer, my proposal photography packages are designed to ensure that every detail is captured flawlessly. Whether it's a surprise proposal or a planned event, I'm here to help you capture the magic of this once-in-a-lifetime moment. Let's work together to create memories that will last a lifetime.

How it works 

To ensure everything goes smoothly, please find out below how the proposal photography process works.

  • Choosing the Location - The first step is selecting the perfect spot for your proposal. If you’re considering a park or public area in Melbourne, please check if you might need a permit, as this can affect our plans. If you’re not familiar with the area, I’m here to help you find the ideal location!
  • Exact Spot - Once you’ve chosen the location, I’ll need the exact spot where you plan to propose. This will help me know where to position myself to capture the moment perfectly.
  • Timing - We’ll discuss the timing of your proposal. If you have plans for a special celebration afterward, like a dinner or lunch, I can help coordinate.
  • Communication - I prefer to communicate via email initially, but as we get closer to the day, I find that messaging through WhatsApp or text is much quicker and more efficient.
  • Selfie & Details - On the day of the proposal, I’ll ask you to send me a selfie of you both or message me details about what you’re wearing. This helps me identify you as you approach and ensures I’m ready to capture the moment.
  • Capturing the Moment - My goal is to stay out of sight until you go down on one knee then I’ll come in closer to photograph the emotions and details of this incredible moment.
  • Post-Proposal Portraits - After the proposal, we’ll follow up with some beautiful portraits to commemorate this special day.

FAQ for Wedding Proposal Photography


What exactly is a proposal?

A wedding proposal is one of the most romantic and unforgettable moments in a couple's journey towards marriage. It's when one partner pops the question to the other, usually alongside a beautiful ring and a heartfelt speech, asking for their hand in marriage. Many people looking to propose to their partner will choose a location or date that has special meaning to them as a couple so choosing to arrange a photographer to capture this special moment is an increasingly popular choice.
